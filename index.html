PURATHAMBI ELLAM, Tamil Nadu

Sangam rules

  • Worry is banned at the tea stall.Bring stories and jokes. Leave the tension at the door.
  • Every member shows up for every other member.Weddings, exams, shifting houses or a flat tyre at midnight.
  • Contribute to the gang.Give your time, ideas and effort for the Sangam.
  • Show respect to Pura and Puna Thambiii.Always, without exception.

Who we are

We are a gang of friends who believe most problems get smaller after tea, a good laugh and a bit of company. The Sangam meets, helps each other out, celebrates every small win and turns up whenever a friend needs a hand.

“Kavalaipadatha. Namma irukkom.”Don’t worry. We’re here.
